BodeaLabs brings together two complementary research groups working at the intersection of molecular neurogenetics and neuroimmunology.

**Cellular Neuroimmunometabolism Dynamics (#CNDynamics)**, led by [Dr. Liviu-Gabriel Bodea](/team/liviu/), investigates microglial biology, protein homeostasis, and metabolic reprogramming in neurological conditions including Alzheimer's disease, frontotemporal dementia, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis.

**Molecular Neurogenetics (MNG)**, led by [Dr. Gabriela O. Bodea](/team/gabriela/), studies retrotransposon biology in neural development, uncovering how mobile genetic elements shape the developing brain.

Together, the two labs share expertise, resources, and a commitment to understanding the molecular mechanisms underlying brain health and disease.
